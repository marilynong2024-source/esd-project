Refund & cancellation policy (summary)
  • Customer: Approved refunds processed within 30 days. Standard flight cancellations: flat S$300 fee on the estimated flight portion. No refund for no-shows (separate no-show fees may apply).
  • Flights: Budget / small-carrier flights are non-refundable. Major airlines: fare less airline cancellation charges (optional fee in API estimate). Airline cancels: 100% refund.
  • Hotels: 100% of estimated hotel portion if you cancel ≥7 days before check-in (we use departure date as proxy); inside 7 days that portion is non-refundable. Hotel cancels: 100% refund; loyalty coins used are restored.
  • Packages: If the airline cancels but the hotel stay could continue, we seek a comparable alternative; if you decline or none is suitable, you receive a full package refund (use “Airline” as cancel source in this demo).
